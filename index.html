Our story · Since 2018

Sweetness with a sense of place.

Messa Marayoor Jaggery began in 2018 in Marayoor, Idukki district, Kerala, with one aim: to make jaggery the hygienic way. Unlike the usual practice across India, every step of our production follows strict standards of hygiene and food safety.

Today we are the only jaggery unit in the region to hold GST, FSSAI, ISO 22000:2018, IEC and APEDA registrations — and a key supplier of export-quality, GI-tagged jaggery.

Our motto is to change the traditional way of jaggery production, and give our customers a healthier and safer option.

Why jaggery

What is Marayoor jaggery? മറയൂർ ശർക്കര

Jaggery, or sharkara, is unrefined cane sugar: sugarcane juice boiled until it sets. It has sweetened Indian kitchens for thousands of years and has a place in Ayurveda. Marayoor jaggery comes only from the Marayoor region of Kerala.
